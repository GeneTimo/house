Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Kingmaker LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, operating under the House of ROI brand (“House of ROI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information through thehouseofroi.com and related pages, forms, and communications (collectively, the “Site”).

This Policy covers information collected for House of ROI's own website, marketing, lead-generation, and business-development purposes. It does not govern personal information we process on behalf of a client under a separate services agreement.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide. We collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Name, work email address, telephone number, company, job title, and location.
  • Website or company-domain information.
  • Service interests, budget range, project timing, and inquiry details.
  • Scheduling information and communications with us.
  • Newsletter or marketing preferences.
  • Feedback, survey responses, and other information you submit.

Do not submit passwords, advertising-account credentials, payment-card details, health information, government identifiers, sensitive personal information, trade secrets, or confidential client data through the Site.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Site, we and our providers automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device type, browser, operating system, language, and screen characteristics.
  • Referring and exit pages, URLs, page views, clicks, scrolling, navigation paths, and timestamps.
  • Advertising identifiers, cookie identifiers, and similar online identifiers.
  • Campaign, referral, conversion, and attribution information.
  • Interactions with forms, pages, and interface elements, including heatmap and session-replay recordings.
  • Email-delivery, open, click, and unsubscribe information, where our communications provider supports it.

Information from other sources. We may receive information from advertising and analytics platforms, social networks, referral sources, publicly available business sources, data-enrichment providers, and vendors.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, and similar technologies in these categories:

  • Necessary: security, fraud prevention, consent-preference storage, and core form or Site functionality.
  • Analytics: understanding traffic, page use, campaign performance, and aggregate trends, including through Google Analytics.
  • Advertising: measuring advertising, attributing conversions, creating or using advertising audiences, and delivering or evaluating ads, including through Google Ads and the Meta Pixel.
  • Experience: understanding interaction patterns through heatmaps and session-replay tools such as Hotjar.

Where required by applicable law, we request your consent through a cookie banner before nonessential technologies are used. Browser settings can also block or delete cookies, but doing so may affect Site functionality.

Do Not Track. The Site does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals. The third parties that provide the tools described above may collect information about your online activities over time and across websites.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • Provide, operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Site.
  • Respond to inquiries, schedule calls, evaluate potential engagements, and manage business relationships.
  • Analyze Site use, user journeys, campaign performance, and conversions.
  • Advertise, retarget, measure, and optimize House of ROI services.
  • Send newsletters and promotional communications in accordance with applicable law.
  • Maintain suppression lists and honor unsubscribe requests.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and unlawful conduct.
  • Enforce our agreements, protect rights, safety, property, and systems, and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Comply with law, legal process, tax, accounting, and recordkeeping obligations.
  • Complete a corporate transaction such as a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets.

We may aggregate or de-identify information and use it for lawful analytics, research, benchmarking, security, and service-improvement purposes.

4. Legal bases for European and UK processing

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar law applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Consent: for nonessential cookies and similar technologies, and for marketing where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time as described in Section 9.
  • Contract and pre-contract steps: to respond to a request, evaluate an engagement, and schedule communications.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate and secure the Site, prevent fraud, manage business relationships, improve our services, and establish or defend claims, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements.

5. How we disclose information

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Hosting, content-delivery, cybersecurity, IT, and website-operation providers.
  • Form, CRM, scheduling, email, and communications providers.
  • Analytics and experience providers, including Google Analytics and Hotjar.
  • Advertising and social-media platforms, including Google and Meta, for measurement, attribution, audience, and advertising purposes.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, auditors, insurers, and legal advisers.
  • Government authorities, courts, regulators, and other persons when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate misconduct, or handle claims.
  • Parties to an actual or proposed merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, sale of assets, or similar transaction.
  • Other parties at your direction or with your consent.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising platforms receive online identifiers and activity data through the tools described in Section 2.

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including managing inquiries and relationships, maintaining business and tax records, honoring unsubscribe requests, securing the Site, and resolving or defending claims. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we maintain. No website, transmission, or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for using appropriate care when deciding what to submit through the Site.
