Rules of the House

01

In God We Trust. Everyone Else Brings Data.

We're proponents of data-driven decisions and have limited tolerance for self-referential advice, speculative best practices, "trust-me-bro" vendor efforts, or methodology without measurable validation.

02

We Tell the Truth.

We're focused on results, not process. Expect to hear the things that agencies prioritizing "client service" and internal politics are too afraid or shy to tell you. We are here to move the needle, not to be liked.

03

We're Not Taking Hostages.

Partnerships continue by performance, not inertia.

04

You Set the Destination. We Fly the Plane.

We'll focus on your objectives, not ours. But we don't operate by committee — we're not here to debate execution with unqualified voices, accommodate vendor agendas, or implement crowd-sourced strategy.

05

We Interview Clients Too.

Every engagement starts with mutual vetting. We evaluate budget adequacy, decision-making speed, organizational readiness, alignment with our cultural and ethical standards, and general market fit. Access is not automatic.
